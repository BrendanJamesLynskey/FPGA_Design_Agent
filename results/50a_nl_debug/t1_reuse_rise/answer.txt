Triggered on win_wr rises. Cycle 0 is sample 4 of 1024 at 100 MHz. The capture holds cycles -4 to 1019; this answer covers cycles -4 to 8, as asked.
At cycle 0: win_wr = 1, count = 0x0000.
win_wr was 1 at the trigger; high for 1 cycle from cycle 0 (1 of 13 samples high).
count was 0x0000 from cycle -4 to 0, then 0x0042 from cycle 1 to 8.
Read back over the shell after the capture: COUNT = 0x00000042.
Supply and temperature from the chip's own monitor (XADC) when armed: die 25.8 C, VCCINT 1.006 V, VCCAUX 1.794 V, VCCBRAM 1.006 V; after upload: die 25.8 C, VCCINT 1.006 V, VCCAUX 1.794 V, VCCBRAM 1.006 V; inside the recommended operating conditions (ROC: the data sheet's supply and temperature ranges).
