Triggered on win_wr rises OR press_clear rises: cycle 0 is sample 8 of 1024 at 100 MHz (the capture holds cycles -8 to 1015; this answer covers cycles -8 to 64 as asked). At cycle 0: win_wr = 1, press_clear = 0, count = 0x0000, duty = 0x40.
win_wr was 1 at the trigger; high for 1 cycle from cycle 0 (1 of 73 samples high).
press_clear held 0 for the whole window (cycles -8 to 64).
count held 0x0000 for the whole window (cycles -8 to 64).
duty was 0x40 from cycle -8 to 0, then 0x22 from cycle 1 to 64.
Operating conditions (XADC over JTAG) armed: die 26.5 C, VCCINT 1.006 V, VCCAUX 1.794 V, VCCBRAM 1.006 V; done: die 26.5 C, VCCINT 1.006 V, VCCAUX 1.794 V, VCCBRAM 1.006 V; inside ROC.
