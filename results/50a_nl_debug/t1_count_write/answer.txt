Triggered on win_wr == 1 AND addr == 0x80. Cycle 0 is sample 16 of 1024 at 100 MHz. The capture holds cycles -16 to 1007; this answer covers cycles -16 to 32, as asked.
At cycle 0: win_wr = 1, addr = 0x80, count = 0x0003.
win_wr was 1 at the trigger; high for 1 cycle from cycle 0 (1 of 49 samples high).
addr held 0x80 for the whole window (cycles -16 to 32).
count was 0x0003 from cycle -16 to 0, then 0x1234 from cycle 1 to 32.
Read back over the shell after the capture: COUNT = 0x00001234.
Supply and temperature from the chip's own monitor (XADC) when armed: die 24.7 C, VCCINT 1.006 V, VCCAUX 1.794 V, VCCBRAM 1.006 V; after upload: die 24.9 C, VCCINT 1.006 V, VCCAUX 1.794 V, VCCBRAM 1.006 V; inside the recommended operating conditions (ROC: the data sheet's supply and temperature ranges).
