Own the signed 16-bit sample and unsigned Q15 gain state. Reset the sample to zero and gain to 2068. A gain write stores min(value, 8192). Produce sample_out combinationally as floor(sample * gain / 32768), represented as signed 16-bit two's complement. SAMPLE, GAIN, and SOUT are the shell-window registers at offsets 0x00, 0x01, and 0x02 respectively; this module's bus: top map uses window_base 0x00.
