# Brief 142: where Yosys's UltraScale+ mapping falls short of Vivado, measured

Written 2026-11-21 (stamped with `date`). Yosys 0.70+385 (OSS CAD Suite, git sha1 098e46a36), `read_slang` front end; Vivado 2026.1; parts xck26-sfvc784-2LV-c (K26) and, as a control, xc7a100t.
Nothing was posted to YosysHQ: the drafts under `drafts/` are for the owner to decide on.

## Result in five lines

1. **Carry (CARRY4 against CARRY8): a gap in the Yosys netlist, not in the placed design.** `synth_xilinx -family xcup` emits CARRY4 only (a 32-bit add: 8 CARRY4 + 32 LUT2, the same as `-family xc7`; Vivado synthesis: 4 CARRY8 + 32 LUT2).
   Vivado 2026.1 converts every CARRY4 of a Yosys EDIF to CARRY8 when it reads it ("Unisim Transformation Summary: (CARRY4) => CARRY8", `vivado/carry4_to_carry8_transform.txt`; 8 CARRY4 become 4 CARRY8, 12 become 6, 18 become 10, 3 become 2), so
   after place and route the carry count equals the draft-CARRY8 netlist's. A hand CARRY8 map in `arith_map.v` (draft change 1) changed no placed result beyond the run-to-run spread (below). Still worth a pull request for the netlist's own sake (estimates read from Yosys output, equivalence tools); the owner decides.
2. **DSP48E2 absorption is the real gap on UltraScale+.** On xcup the multiplier maps to the DSP but the accumulate feedback, pattern detect, pre-adder and SIMD do not (`m14_mac`: 48 LUT + 12 CARRY4 against 0 LUT in Vivado; the same source on `-family xc7` gives 1 DSP and 0 LUT). After place and route, `m14_mac` is WNS -0.397 ns (Yosys) against +1.285 ns (Vivado), `m11_mult18` -0.277 against +1.305 (Yosys keeps 108 fabric flip-flops where Vivado uses the DSP's own registers, 0).
3. **Hard muxes and LUT packing: Vivado is smaller.** Without `-widemux` Yosys uses no MUXF7/F8: a 16:1 mux per bit over 16 bits is 106 LUT against 64 LUT + 32 MUXF7 + 16 MUXF8; `-widemux 5` does not fix it (`m34_mux8_case`: 64 LUT + 32 F7 + 16 F8 against Vivado's 32 LUT + 16 F7; `k03_lg_small_comb`: 458 LUT + 262 F7 + 181 F8 against 330 LUT) and stops with an error on two registered arithmetic blocks (draft issue 4). Real blocks: CRC-64 network 308 LUT against 213, `r07_mult_booth18` 781 against 510, `r06_mult_lutarray18` 725 against 543 (synthesis stage).
4. **Memories and shift registers.** BRAM and URAM inference matches Vivado's primitive counts when asked for (`-uram` is opt-in; `m22_bram_big` becomes 4 URAM with it), but the output register of a block RAM stays in 32 fabric flip-flops (`m20_bram_sdp`: Vivado 0 FF), and a shift register read at a run-time tap is 114 LUT + 264 FF against 8 SRLC32E (draft issue 2). Control sets (CE, reset, async reset) are counted equal on the cases tried.
5. **Place and route does not erase the netlist differences:** the Yosys arm is larger on all three real blocks taken through place and route (`c01_cordic_iterative` 204 LUT against 162, `k02_pcs_tx` 770 against 758, `k04_adder_tree8` 81 against 61) and has the worse WNS on 4 of the 6 points (`c01`, `k02`, `m11`, `m14`), the better on `k04_adder_tree8` (+0.525 against +0.412) and equal on `m01_add32` (single runs; the spread on one identical netlist is about 0.3 ns, see below). So the agent should take UltraScale+ structural netlists and resource and timing estimates from Vivado, not from Yosys (KB claim, `drafts/kb_claim.md`).

## What was measured

* Benchmark set (`rtl/`, `scripts/manifest.py`): 38 micro cases (one feature each) and 23 real blocks: the arithmetic library of briefs 139a and 139b (adders, multipliers, MACs, divider, square root, normaliser, rounder), four CORDIC points of brief 137, four K26 blocks of brief 101 (CRC-64, PCS transmit, a combinational block, an adder tree).
* Yosys (`scripts/yosys_run.py`, `yosys/`): `synth_xilinx -family xcup` and `-family xc7`, variants `def`, `flat` (`-flatten`), `tuned` (`-flatten -widemux 5`, plus `-uram` on xcup), `dff` (`-flatten -dff`), and on xcup `c8` (flat plus the draft CARRY8 map). abc9 is the default LUT mapper in this Yosys, so there is no separate abc9 arm. 549 runs; cell counts in `summary.json`, logs in `yosys/`. The Yosys JSON netlists are not committed (53 MB); rerun the script to regenerate them.
* Vivado (`scripts/vivado_ooc.tcl`, `scripts/vivado_jobs.py`, `vivado/`): out-of-context synthesis, one thread, default directives, reports at the synthesis stage for 55 of the 61 K26 cases and 14 of the 38 micro cases on xc7a100t, and place and route for 6 designs x 3 arms (Vivado's own synthesis; the Yosys flat EDIF; the Yosys EDIF with the draft CARRY8 map).
* Tables: `tables.md` (primitive by primitive, K26 against Yosys xcup, xc7a100t control), `impl_table.md` (place and route: LUT, FF, CARRY8, DSP, WNS, Fmax = 1000 / (2.5 - WNS)). Both are generated by scripts from the recorded reports.

## Run-to-run spread

The same placed netlist differs between arms in `m01_add32`: the flat and draft-CARRY8 Yosys netlists are identical after Vivado's CARRY4-to-CARRY8 conversion (4 CARRY8, 32 LUT2, 96 FF) yet give WNS +0.943 and +1.242 ns. Differences under about 0.3 ns in `impl_table.md` are therefore not evidence of either arm being better. The gaps above (DSP absorption, hundreds of LUTs) are far outside that.

## Known gaps and limits

* Vivado synthesis-stage reports are missing for six K26 cases (`r02_adder_pipelined32x4`, `r03_adder_csel32`, `r12_div_iter16`, `r14_lznorm_tree32`, `c02_cordic_unrolled_k_k4`, `c04_cordic_pipelined_m_m3`) and for 24 of the 38 micro cases on xc7a100t (the control used 14): the first run of the batch stopped part-way (`hs_err` log not kept) and a second batch covered the rest of the planned set. The table shows `n/a` for them.
* Place and route covers 6 designs, one run per arm (the brief asked for "a few points"); no repeat runs except the incidental one in the spread section.
* Vivado's logic-levels figure is read from the synthesis-stage worst path, unplaced; it has no Yosys counterpart.
* `abc9` boxes: the draft CARRY8 map gives `abc9` no carry delay through the new cells (`cells_sim.v` has no timing for CARRY8); noted in `drafts/pr_1/README.md`.
* The draft pull request was tested through the installed Yosys driven by hand with the same commands the patched `synth_xilinx` would run; the C++ change was never compiled (no cmake on this machine). `-family xcup` runs of `add_sub_xcup.ys` need the rebuilt binary.
* Not covered by the brief's list, not measured: cascaded DSPs across columns beyond `r09_mac_cascade4` (Vivado 5 DSP + 40 LUT against Yosys 4 DSP + 139 LUT, 37 CARRY4), URAM cascades, flip-flop control-set packing beyond `m28` to `m30`.

## Reproduce

Needs the OSS CAD Suite (Yosys 0.70+385 with the slang plugin) at `~/.local/opt/oss-cad-suite` (or `FDA_OSS_CAD`) and Vivado 2026.1 (run in a heavy slot; one thread, under `systemd-run --scope -p MemoryMax=6G`).

```
python3 results/b142/scripts/yosys_run.py                 # Yosys runs, writes yosys/<family>/<variant>/
python3 results/b142/scripts/vivado_jobs.py ...            # writes the job files for scripts/vivado_ooc.tcl (see its docstring); run_vivado_142.sh takes the slot and runs them
bash results/b142/scripts/collect_vivado.sh <scratch dir>  # copies the small reports into vivado/
python3 results/b142/scripts/summarize.py && python3 results/b142/scripts/tables.py && python3 results/b142/scripts/impl_table.py
```
