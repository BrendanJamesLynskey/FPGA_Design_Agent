## T1 result (local run, 2026-11-01)

T1 reaches **V3** (V0, V1, V2, V3 pass); V-HW passes from the committed bring-up result (`results/f0b`).

- **V1:** 26 of 26 spec items traced to 21 requirements; no unresolved reference.
- **V2:** 15 cocotb tests (9 from F0A, 6 new including the coverage dump) pass on seeds 1, 2, 3 under Verilator 5.048 with the bound
  checkers (23 assertions, zero failures) and under Icarus 12; SymbiYosys bounded proof (depth 24 and 40) and cover
  pass on the debouncer and the display scanner.
- **V3:** functional bins 69/69 (100 %); cover properties 100 %; line 100 % (36/36); toggle 87.24 % (759/870;
  threshold 80 %); branch 100 %. The 111 unhit toggle points are structural: the upper bits of `win_rdata` that no
  register drives (the spec reads them as 0), and `an[5:4]` (the blank digits). No waivers were needed.
- **Mutation:** 21 mutants (operator, constant, condition, reset). **First full run: 19 of 21 killed (90.48 %) by
  the bench alone; the bound checkers killed none that the bench missed.** Survivors: `db_reset_level` (the
  debounced level resets to 1) and `scan_reset_an` (the anodes reset all low): both are reset-state faults no
  F0A/F2 test looked at. A test was written for them (`reset_state_of_outputs`); with it both are killed, final
  **21 of 21 (100 %)**. The 90.48 % is the unbiased figure; the 100 % is after fixing the bench for the two
  survivors, and 19 of its 21 rows come from the first run (a new test cannot un-kill a mutant).
