# results/b100b: brief 100B, can RapidWright match or beat Vivado's compile time?

Vivado 2026.1 (Basic licence), RapidWright 2026.1.2, `xck26-sfvc784-2LV-c` (K26) and `xc7a100tcsg324-1` (Artix-7), one thread, every job in its own
systemd scope (6 GB cap), heavy slot held, no other Vivado on the PC (each job records the load average at its start and end). Machine: i7-3770, 32 GB.
The report is `_handoff/fpga_agent/reports/100B_rw_compile_speed_report.md`; the numbers are in `summary.md` / `summary.json`
(generated by `flow/rw/speed/summarise.py` from the per-job records here) and `where_time.md` (generated by `flow/rw/speed/where_time.py`).

## Records

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `where_time.md`, `where_time.json` | step 1: where the time goes, from existing run records (no new run) |
| `summary.md`, `summary.json` | experiments 1 and 2, mean / min / max over three repetitions, Vivado's WNS and route status as the referee |
| `e1/` | one-block edit: `F` Vivado full rebuild, `I` Vivado incremental (refused by the licence), `Sblk` + `Sstamp` stitch, `S2rw` RWRoute completion (fails) |
| `e2_k26/`, `e2_a7/` | the router: `V` Vivado `route_design`, `RT` RWRoute timing-driven, `RN` RWRoute not timing-driven, `*ref` Vivado timing of the RWRoute result |

Per job: `<tag>.meas.json` (wall, CPU, peak memory, load), `<tag>_res_run.json` (Vivado's WNS/WHS and stage laps), `<tag>_res_route_status.txt`,
`<tag>_res_timing.rpt`, `<tag>.log.tail` (last 25 lines of the log). Checkpoints are not committed (they are in `~/Claude_sandbox/_worktrees/100b-work/`).

## Reproduce

    tools/rapidwright_env/setup.sh                                   # RapidWright venv (git-ignored)
    # inputs: the blocks and the placed K26 design of brief 100's capability 1 (designs/b100_rapidwright/scripts/run_c1.sh) in $W/in
    flow/rw/speed/run_e1.sh                                          # experiment 1, about 1 hour 10 minutes
    flow/rw/speed/run_e2.sh k26 $W/in/alone_p3_40_placed.dcp 3.4 3.7 4.0   # experiment 2, about 1 hour 10 minutes
    flow/rw/speed/run_e2_a7.sh                                       # Artix-7 (RWRoute refuses it), about 40 minutes
    python3 flow/rw/speed/summarise.py; python3 flow/rw/speed/where_time.py

Take a heavy slot first. Wall times depend on the machine; the quality numbers (WNS) repeated exactly in every repetition.

## Known gaps and limits

- Vivado's incremental implementation is refused by the Basic licence, so the fair rival of the stitch was not measured.
- One small design (3,296 LUT), one kind of edit, one placement for the router comparison; repetitions repeat identical inputs.
- RWRoute's partial `complete` mode crashes on the stamped checkpoint (not debugged); RWRoute refuses Series-7 parts.
- No eqy equivalence run of the stamped design; nothing wired into the agent flow.
