Memory: the peak RSS is the largest single Vivado process (`/usr/bin/time -v`). The scope figures come from the build's cgroup, sampled every 2 s: anonymous memory summed over every process in it (the ILA build starts helper Vivado processes to synthesise the debug cores), and the charged total, which also counts page cache. When the charged total reaches the 6 GB cap the kernel reclaims cache first; a build is at risk only if anonymous memory approaches the cap or an OOM kill is recorded.
