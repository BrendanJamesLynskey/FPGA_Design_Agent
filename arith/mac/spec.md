# Multiply-accumulate (dot-product accumulator) (arith/mac)

Generated by `python -m arith.tools.gen_spec_md` from [spec.yaml](spec.yaml); edit the YAML, not this file. Part of the arithmetic library of brief 139a ([../README.md](../README.md)).

A multiply-accumulate unit with LANES products summed per clock into one accumulator: acc <= (clr ? 0 : acc) + sum of a_k * b_k when en = 1. Three implementations trade DSP blocks against fabric and latency: a DSP-mapped MAC with 1 to 3 register stages, a chain of LANES DSPs joined like a cascade, and an all-fabric MAC.

## Function

- on a clock with en = 1: acc <= (clr ? 0 : acc) + the sum over the lanes of a_k * b_k, reduced modulo 2**ACCW; with en = 0 acc holds
- rst (synchronous) sets acc to 0 and discards every sample still in the pipeline, including the one presented on the same clock
- the products are signed (SIGNED = 1) or unsigned and are sign- or zero-extended to ACCW bits before they are added; there is no saturation
- acc is always the sum of the products since the last clr, modulo 2**ACCW, for any run length (Lean: ArithMac.run_noclr, ArithMac.run_after_clr)

Signedness: SIGNED chooses the reading of every lane and of acc; the products are sign- (or zero-) extended to ACCW before they are added.

Exactness: exact modulo 2**ACCW: acc equals the integer sum of the products since the last clr, reduced modulo 2**ACCW; no rounding, no saturation.

Latency: clocks from a sample being presented (en = 1) to acc including it; 1 means the same edge, as for a plain registered accumulator; one sample per clock in every variant.

## Ports

| Port | Direction | Width | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clk` | in | 1 | clock |
| `rst` | in | 1 | synchronous reset: clears acc and every sample in flight, including the sample presented on the same clock |

| `b` | in | LANES*WB | LANES multipliers, lane k in bits [k*WB +: WB] |
| `acc` | out | ACCW | the accumulator, registered, modulo 2**ACCW: it wraps, it does not saturate |

| `WA` | min 1 | width of each a lane |

### Intended primitive use

- `dsp`, Artix-7 (xc7a100t): DSP48E1: LANES (WA <= 25, WB <= 18 signed); accumulate in the DSP for LANES = 1; registers absorbed at NREG = 3
  - K26 (xck26): DSP48E2 (27 x 18): LANES; accumulate in the DSP for LANES = 1
- `dsp_cascade`, Artix-7 (xc7a100t): DSP48E1: LANES chained PCOUT to PCIN, plus the accumulate in fabric or one DSP C-port add; skew registers (about (WA+WB)*LANES*(LANES-1)/2 FF or SRL)
  - K26 (xck26): DSP48E2: as a7
- `fabric`, Artix-7 (xc7a100t): 0 DSP; about LANES*WA*WB LUTs; LANES*WB carry chains up to ACCW bits; ACCW FF (2*ACCW at NREG = 2)
  - K26 (xck26): 0 DSP; as a7 with CARRY8 cells

## Interface contract

Written without knowing the design the block will sit in (brief 139a, owner decision 24): this is what a user can rely on and must provide.

- Protocol: plain registered ports with an enable: a sample (a, b, clr) is accepted on every clock where en = 1 and ignored where en = 0; no ready or backpressure (the block can always accept). The result is the registered accumulator, always valid after reset, and it changes only when a sample reaches the accumulator, latency clocks after it was presented
- Throughput: one sample (LANES products) per clock in every variant (initiation interval 1)
- Latency: per variant, in clocks: dsp NREG (1 to 3), dsp_cascade LANES + 1, fabric NREG (1 or 2); en and clr travel with the data, so a result can be matched to its sample by counting clocks
- Reset: synchronous rst, active high: clears acc and discards every sample in flight (including one presented on the same clock); hold it for at least one clock; the data registers inside the pipeline are not reset (only the enable pipeline is), which is harmless because a discarded sample has its enable cleared. Reset is needed once after power-up: the accumulator has no initial value
- Clock: one clock, rising edge; no clock gating; en is the only throttle
- Stalls: an en = 0 clock is a bubble: the accumulator holds; there is no way to freeze the pipeline stages themselves, so a sample already in flight completes

|---|---|---|
| `dsp` | `NREG` | 1 per clock |
| `dsp_cascade` | `LANES + 1` | 1 per clock |
| `fabric` | `NREG` | 1 per clock |

## Integration

- Port timing: all variants register the accumulator, so acc can be read by ordinary registers with a full clock of slack. Inputs: with NREG >= 2 (dsp, fabric) and for dsp_cascade the operands are registered inside, so they can be driven by ordinary registers; dsp with NREG 1 and fabric with NREG 1 leave the whole multiply-and-add as a combinational path from a/b to acc, a long path the caller must budget
- Constraints to carry: no multicycle or false paths, one clock. DSP variants: keep operand, product and accumulator registers in the DSP (NREG 3); the accumulator feedback stays inside one DSP when LANES = 1, so do not insert logic between acc and its consumer that forces the P register out of the DSP (a second reader of acc is fine; a mux on acc is not). The synchronous reset and enable map onto the DSP's RSTP and CEP pins; an asynchronous reset would defeat that (FDA-HDL HDL-RST-03)
- Verification after integration: rerun the packaged harness against your instance ([harness/README.md](harness/README.md)); it needs no other file of this repository.

## What a user could guess wrong

- acc wraps modulo 2**ACCW and never saturates: a run of N products of full-scale operands needs about log2(N) guard bits beyond WA + WB
- clr does not clear acc to 0 on its own: it makes THIS clock's sample the first of a new sum (acc becomes the product, not 0); a sample with en = 0 ignores clr
- rst discards the samples in the pipeline as well as clearing acc; with latency L, a sample presented fewer than L clocks before rst is lost
- the output acc lags the input by the latency, and the variants differ in it (NREG, LANES + 1): compare results by latency, not by clock number
- with SIGNED = 1 every lane of a and b is signed; there is no mixed-signedness lane
- dsp_cascade needs its inputs for all lanes on the same clock: it delays the higher lanes itself, so the caller does not skew them
- ACCW must be at least WA + WB (the model and the tests assume it) and LANES at least 1; NREG above the stated range behaves as the maximum (3 for dsp, 2 for fabric); none of this is rejected by an assertion
- the data registers inside the pipeline are not reset: only en is, so after rst the accumulator is 0 and stays 0 until a new sample with en = 1 arrives
