DIVNR-F-LEAN-GS: The exact Goldschmidt iteration keeps the ratio and squares the denominator's distance from 1 (Lean)

For all integers: multiplying numerator and denominator by the same factor keeps n*b = d*a (so the numerator is the quotient when the denominator reaches 1.0), and the denominator update d(2S - d) has S*S - d*(2S - d) = (S - d)**2, hence never exceeds 1.0 once d <= S. Proved in Lean, statements not approved; the truncations of the RTL and the seed table's accuracy are not in Lean (they are measured and proved at fixed widths by SymbiYosys).

  • Lean proof (all widths): statement not approved Goldschmidt keeps the ratio: n*b = d*a implies (n*r)*b = (d*r)*a, every integer value: statement not approved; Arith.Newton.gs_ratio_invariant: kernel only; proof of a model, no RTL link; recorded build (not re-run) evidence
  • Lean proof (all widths): statement not approved The denominator update squares its distance from 1: S*S - d*(2S - d) = (S - d)**2: statement not approved; Arith.Newton.gs_denominator_residual: kernel only; proof of a model, no RTL link; recorded build (not re-run) evidence
