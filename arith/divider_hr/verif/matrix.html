DIVHR-F-FORMAL-LEMMA: The digit-recurrence step lemmas hold for all inputs (SymbiYosys)

SymbiYosys with the slang front end, the combinational step lemmas of formal/divider_hr_lemma_fv.sv for ALL inputs at a fixed width (6 widths, W = 7 to 16): L1 the first digit is 0, 1 or 2 and leaves |a - q*b| <= 2b/3; L2 the carry-save step, for ANY two words whose sum is bounded, sums to 4u - q*b and is bounded; L3 the same for the carry-propagate step; L4 the on-the-fly conversion keeps QM = Q - 1; L5 the fix returns the remainder in [0, b) and the right quotient. With the induction on the number of steps (proved in Lean for every K, DIVHR-F-LEAN-RECURRENCE) these are the correctness argument of the recurrence at that width.

DIVHR-F-LEAN-RECURRENCE: The SRT recurrence with any contained digit selection is a floor division for every K (Lean)

For every number of digit steps K and every digit-selection rule that keeps the partial remainder bounded (|u| <= 2b/3, written 3|u| <= 2b), after K steps b * Q + U = 4**K * (b * q1 + u1) with U bounded, and the correction (Q - 1 and U + b when U < 0) is exactly floor(4**K * a / b) and 4**K * a mod b, with the remainder in [0, b). Proved in Lean, statements not approved; the digit-selection table's containment is NOT in Lean (it is an exact-fraction search in the model and the step lemmas above), and registers, widths, the carry-save form and the on-the-fly conversion are not modelled.
