# AXI4-Stream 2-parallel FFT with block floating point (wrapper of fft_mdc2, N = 16 to 4096, exponent on tuser) (arith/fft_mdc2_axis)

Generated by `python -m arith.tools.gen_spec_md` from [spec.yaml](spec.yaml); edit the YAML, not this file. Part of the frequency-domain suite of brief 168 ([../README.md](../README.md)).

The 2-parallel streaming FFT (arith/fft_mdc2: two complex samples per clock) behind AMBA AXI4-Stream ports (owner decision 28), with the output as a block-floating-point frame (the output form of arith/fft_bfp): a slave stream of frames of N complex samples, two per beat, with the direction on tuser of the first beat, and a master stream of the N-point spectrum, two bins per beat (X[k] and X[k + N/2] at beat k, natural order), every part W bits, with the frame's block exponent on tuser. The transform inside is the unscaled one (W + LOGN bit parts); an NB-bank frame store behind it (NB = 4 by default) finds the exponent (the bit length of the OR of all magnitudes, as in arith/fft_bfp) and releases the frame shifted to W bits. The wrapper handles the two streaming rules of the core itself: the frame gap (0 or at least N/8 idle clocks, enforced by tready) and the output frame store (at most NB frames in the block, enforced by tready), so the sink may stall without limit and the source may pause between frames without breaking the core.

## Function

- input: beats of s_axis_tdata = {im1, re1, im0, re0}, W bits each (not padded to a byte boundary, so s_axis_tdata is 4 * W bits), two's complement; sample 0 = x[2m] (EVEN index) is in the LOW half and sample 1 = x[2m + 1] in the HIGH half; a frame is N/2 beats, tlast high on the N/2-th; s_axis_tuser[0] = 1 selects the inverse transform and is read on the FIRST beat of a frame only (the core samples the direction with the start of frame; the later beats' tuser is ignored)
- a beat is transferred when tvalid and tready are both high in a clock (AXI4-Stream handshake); s_axis_tready and m_axis_tvalid depend only on registers, never on the other side's valid or ready combinationally except m_axis_tready into the credit counter's register input; the wrapped core never sees a beat that was not transferred
- the transform: forward (tuser 0) X[k] = sum x[n] exp(-j 2 pi n k / N), inverse (tuser 1) exp(+j ...), both WITHOUT the 1/N factor, computed unscaled in W + LOGN bit parts (arith/fft_mdc2 at SCALE = 0, ORDER = 1) and then normalised per frame as a block-floating-point frame: s = BL + 1 - W with BL the bit length of the OR of the ones-complement magnitudes of every part of every output sample (x for x >= 0, -x - 1 for x < 0), every part shifted right by s (rounded half up when RND = 1, floored when RND = 0; a negative s is a left shift, exact) and a positive part that the rounding carries to 2**(W - 1) saturated to 2**(W - 1) - 1; the true value of every part is part * 2**s, to within arith/fft_bfp's stated error. The bit-exact model is arith/fft_mdc2_axis/model.py (fft_ref.mdc2_frame at SCALE = 0, then fft_ref.bfp_normalise)
- output: beats of m_axis_tdata = {im1, re1, im0, re0}, W bits each; beat k = 0 .. N/2 - 1 carries sample 0 = X[k] (the lower half of the spectrum) in the LOW half and sample 1 = X[k + N/2] in the HIGH half, in natural order; tlast on the N/2-th beat. m_axis_tuser = the block exponent s, signed, EW = $clog2(2 * W + LOGN) + 1 bits (two's complement), valid on the FIRST beat of the frame and, by this implementation, held at the same value on every beat of the frame (a sink may latch it on the first beat, which is the contract, or read it on any beat)
- why the first beat carries the exponent: the exponent depends on every output sample, so it is known only when the last output sample of the frame has left the core, which is before the first beat of the frame is released from the frame store; the first beat is therefore the earliest beat that can carry it and a downstream scaler can latch it before any data of the frame arrives (carrying it on the last beat would need the exponent to be remembered or the data to be delayed again)
- frame gap (enforced by the wrapper): the core accepts the next frame with no idle clock after the last beat of the previous one, or after at least N/8 idle clocks, never after 1 to N/8 - 1 (arith/fft_mdc2/spec.md). s_axis_tready is high in the one clock after a beat with tlast; a beat transferred then follows with no gap; if no beat is transferred in that clock, s_axis_tready falls for N/8 - 1 clocks and rises again, so that the first beat of the next frame comes N/8 clocks or more after the last beat of the previous one. A source that idles 1 to N/8 - 1 clocks between frames is therefore held off until the gap is N/8 (no frame is corrupted); a source that presents the next frame at once streams back to back
- frame credit (enforced by the wrapper): the output frame store has NB banks (default 4), one per frame in the block. A frame counts from the transfer of its first input beat until the transfer of its last output beat; while NB frames count, s_axis_tready is low at the start of a further frame. The core's own output cannot be stalled, so this credit is what makes a store overflow impossible for any sink. With a sink that is always ready and a source that never pauses, the frame period is N/2 clocks (no idle clock between frames) whenever NB * N/2 >= latency + N/2 (a frame is in the block for the latency plus the N/2 clocks of its output), which holds for NB = 4 from N = 32 on at the default parameters; with fewer banks the credit holds the source and the missed window costs more (NB = 3: periods N/2 and a longer one, 32 and 51 clocks at N = 64, 128 and 160 at N = 256, 512 and 640 at N = 1024; NB = 2: periods up to about 2.2 to 3 times N/2 at N = 16 to 256); the rule and the periods are measured at every point by tests/fft_mdc2_axis_cocotb.py `directed`
- back-pressure inside a frame: none on the input (s_axis_tready is high inside a frame, and tvalid must stay high from the first to the last beat of a frame: the checker flags a pause); the output may be stalled at any beat for any length (m_axis_tvalid, tdata, tuser and tlast hold until taken)
- AXI4-Lite: none: there are no run-time registers (the direction is per frame on tuser; every other option is a compile-time parameter). Optional AXI4-Stream signals not used: tkeep, tstrb, tid, tdest. Compared with arith/fft_axis: this block has two samples per beat, a stallable output and the exponent on the output tuser; it has no SCALE (the block exponent replaces it) and no ORDER (the output is always natural order)

Signedness: two's complement parts inside tdata; tuser of the output is a two's complement exponent.

Exactness: bit-exact against the model (arith/fft_mdc2_axis/model.py: the 2-parallel unscaled transform of arith/fft_mdc2 and the block-floating-point normalisation of arith/fft_common/fft_ref.py); the accuracy against NumPy is that of arith/fft_mdc2 at SCALE = 0 followed by the BFP rounding, measured for the single-sample form in arith/fft_bfp (docs/fft_suite_plan.md section 9).

Latency: clocks from the clock in which the first beat of a frame is transferred on s_axis to the clock in which the first beat of the output frame is offered on m_axis, for a sink that is ready: the core's in_sof to out_sof latency (bit-reversed order) plus N/2 (the frame store collects the whole frame to find its exponent) plus 2 (memory read and shift register).

## Parameters

| Parameter | Constraint | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `LOGN` | min 4 | log2 of the frame length N: 4 to 12 (N = 16 to 4096) |
| `W` | min 4 | input and output part width |
| `TW` | min 4 | twiddle width, 4 to 28 |
| `RND` | one of [0, 1] | 1: round half up at every shift; 0: truncate (floor) |
| `CMUL` | one of [3, 4] | twiddle multiplier form (identical results) |
| `TWSTAGES` | one of [1, 2] | twiddle ROM latency |
| `MULSTAGES` | min 0 | register ranks per complex multiplier, 0 to 3 |
| `NB` | 2 to 4 | output frame banks (default 4): at most NB frames are in the block at once; 4 sustains one frame per N/2 clocks back to back with a ready sink, 3 gives alternating periods of N/2 and up to about N/2 + N/4, 2 roughly a third to half of the rate (measured in tests/fft_mdc2_axis_cocotb.py `directed`, recorded in verif/results) |

### Intended primitive use

- `par2`, Artix-7 (xc7a100t): N = 1024, W = TW = 16, CMUL 4, MULSTAGES 3: arith/fft_mdc2's (9 complex multipliers = 36 DSP48E1, 2 x 511 words of delay memory, 5 to 8 k LUT, 6 to 9 k flip-flops) plus the frame store: NB = 4 banks x 512 words x 104 bits = 208 Kbit: about 10 to 12 RAMB36E1 (a 104-bit word at 512 deep is two 72-bit-wide RAMB36 and one RAMB18 per bank), and a 4-wide shifter (about 4 * 500 LUT); about 50 flip-flops of control; expected Fmax 250 to 350 MHz
  - K26 (xck26): as a7 with DSP48E2 and the store in 2 URAM288 (144 bits x 4096 deep side by side) or 10 to 12 RAMB36; Fmax 400 to 550 MHz expected

## Interface contract

Written without knowing the design the block will sit in (brief 168, owner decision 24): this is what a user can rely on and must provide.

- Protocol: AMBA AXI4-Stream on both interfaces. Slave: frames of N/2 beats, tlast on the last, tvalid held high from the first to the last beat of a frame; tready is low only between frames (the gap rule and the 3-frame credit). Master: frames of N/2 beats, tlast on the last, tuser = the exponent, the sink may stall at any beat (tvalid, tdata, tuser and tlast hold)
- Throughput: 2 samples per clock inside a frame; between frames no idle clock is needed (0 idle clocks) when the sink keeps up and the 3-frame credit allows (measured period in verif/results); a source that idles 1 to N/8 - 1 clocks loses up to N/8 clocks; a sink that stalls for T clocks delays later frames by T clocks once NB frames are in the block
- Latency: N/2 - 1 + (LOGN - 1) + ((LOGN - 2) // 2) * (TWSTAGES + MULSTAGES + 1) + TWSTAGES + MULSTAGES + 2 + N/2 + 2
- Reset: aresetn low for at least one clock clears the control state of the wrapper (start flag, gap window and counter, frame credit), of the core and of the frame store (bank pointers and full flags; the data memories are not cleared); a frame in flight is lost; the first frame after reset starts with its first beat with no gap
- Clock: one clock, rising edge, no clock enable
- Stalls: the output can be stalled at any time; the input is back-pressured between frames only (gap rule, credit); neither stream can be paused inside an input frame

| Variant | Latency (clocks) | Throughput |
|---|---|---|
| `par2` | `(1<<LOGN)//2-1+(LOGN-1)+((LOGN-2)//2)*(TWSTAGES+MULSTAGES+1)+TWSTAGES+MULSTAGES+2+(1<<LOGN)//2+2` | 2 samples per clock; a frame every N/2 clocks at best (back to back, sink ready, credit permitting); the exponent adds no clocks per frame, only latency |

## Integration

- Port timing: m_axis_* come from registers (the output stage's shifter registers); s_axis_tready is a register decode (a LOGN-bit zero detect, the gap window and the credit compare), never combinational from tvalid; m_axis_tready goes to the frame store's read-pipeline enable (a fan-out to about 4 * W + EW registers and the read counter) and to the credit counter; s_axis_tdata goes straight into the core's four input ports
- Constraints to carry: one clock; the protocol checkers sit between `ifndef SYNTHESIS lines and are not part of the design. A sink that can stall must still take every frame eventually: the credit then holds the source, which is the intended back-pressure. The source must keep tvalid high from the first to the last beat of a frame
- Verification after integration: this function has no packaged harness (brief 168 left it out; a later brief can package it as brief 161 did for fft_bfp, fft_mdc2, fft_real_axis and fft_fastconv): rerun its bench (tests/) against your instance, which needs the repository's arith/tools and fft_common helpers.

## What a user could guess wrong

- TWO samples per beat on BOTH streams, not one: s_axis_tdata = {im1, re1, im0, re0} with sample 0 = x[2m] (the EVEN-indexed one) in the LOW half; a source that sends x[m] and x[m + N/2] in one beat is wrong. m_axis beat k carries X[k] in the LOW half and X[k + N/2] in the HIGH half; a frame is N/2 beats, not N. Within a sample the real part is the LOW half and the imaginary part the HIGH half
- the output is a BLOCK-FLOATING-POINT frame: every part is W bits but the true value is part * 2**s with s the exponent on m_axis_tuser; s is NOT a fixed scale and is not 0 for a full-scale frame (a frame whose largest part needs BL magnitude bits has s = BL + 1 - W, so a quiet frame has a negative s: its parts are shifted LEFT, exactly). There is no 1/N: an impulse of height A in a frame with the exponent s_f gives parts of height A * 2**-s_f. A sink that ignores tuser sees a different scale on every frame
- the exponent is on the FIRST beat of the frame (and held on the others in this implementation, which a sink must not rely on if it also targets another implementation); it is two's complement in EW = $clog2(2 * W + LOGN) + 1 bits, so for W = 16, LOGN = 10 it is 7 bits wide. tuser of the INPUT is the direction (bit 0), read on the first beat only
- tready is low in two situations that do not mean the block is broken: for N/8 - 1 clocks when the source did not start the next frame in the clock right after a tlast beat (the gap rule: 0 or at least N/8 idle clocks), and while NB frames are in the block (the sink is slow). A source must not time out on it. The clock right after a tlast beat is the one chance for a back-to-back frame; a source that pauses one clock there waits N/8
- inside a frame there is NO back-pressure: a source that lowers tvalid in the middle of a frame corrupts that frame and the next (nothing flags it in the hardware; the simulation checker does). The OUTPUT can stall, but the input frames still go through the core in streaming mode: only the frame store is behind the stall
- the result is not bit-identical to arith/fft_bfp's (which is built on the single-sample cores): the rounding points of the 2-parallel transform differ (arith/fft_mdc2: two N/2-point transforms, one rounded product); both are checked against their own bit-exact models. The inverse transform is the swap trick of arith/fft (real and imaginary parts exchanged at the input and at every output part), not a conjugated twiddle
- tlast on the input is used only to find where the next frame starts, start the gap window and count the frame; a frame of other than N/2 beats is outside the contract (not flagged, not recovered from). There is no status stream and no tlast_missing or tlast_unexpected event (AMD's FFT has them), no overflow flag (a full-scale corner of the input can wrap inside the core, as in arith/fft_mdc2), and no run-time N
- the SystemVerilog protocol checkers (axis_checker.sv) are simulation-only immediate assertions inside the RTL (between `ifndef SYNTHESIS lines); the concurrent SVA properties of the control logic are in formal/ and are proved with SymbiYosys, they are not part of the design

## Lean, and what is research

Not attempted: the wrapper is control logic around verified blocks; the gap, credit and tlast rules are checked by simulation (every run measures the idle clocks between frames) and the control module by the SymbiYosys jobs in formal/, not proved in Lean.
