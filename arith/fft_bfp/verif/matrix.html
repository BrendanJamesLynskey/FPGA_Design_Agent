Requirements in full

FBFP-F-MODEL: The bit-exact model is right

arith/fft_bfp/model.py gives, for every input, the result that spec.md defines. It agrees with an independent formulation (NumPy's FFT (the value of every word times 2**exponent against the transform, within the stated bound, both variants, N = 8 to 1024, three input levels), a brute-force search for the smallest shift after which every part fits, and the identities of the order and of the inverse), and the conventions that a user could guess wrong (spec.md, last section) are pinned by examples.

  • simulation test: pass The model against independent formulations and the pinned conventions: 71 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence

FBFP-F-EXACT: Every variant is bit-exact against the model

At every parameter point listed in spec.yaml (10 directed or exhaustive points, 6 random ones, across 2 variants), each variant's outputs equal the model's: the directed points drive impulses at every position and tones at every bin (small N), DC, Nyquist, a chirp and corner values, forward and inverse, back to back and after the shortest legal gap; the random points drive random frames with random direction and legal gaps up to N = 4096 (sdf) and 1024 (burst); the level frames drive all-zero, one-LSB, quiet and loud noise, loud and quiet tones, the most negative value and a frame whose rounding carries and saturates, so that every exponent range is visited. Each output frame, its exponent word included, equals the model bit for bit, the latency from in_sof to out_sof equals the formula, out_valid is N consecutive clocks and the exponent is the same on every word of a frame.

  • simulation test: pass Every variant against the model at the small points (Icarus + cocotb): directed or exhaustive stimuli: 10 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence
  • simulation test: pass Every variant against the model at the large points: random stimuli (Icarus + cocotb): 6 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence

FBFP-F-LAT: Latency is as specified and the bench would notice if it were not

Each variant's latency in clocks is the expression in spec.yaml (sdf: (1<<LOGN)-1+LOGN+((LOGN-1)//2)*(TWSTAGES+MULSTAGES+1)+(1<<LOGN)+2; burst: (1<<LOGN)+LOGN*((1<<LOGN)//2+MULSTAGES+3)+1+(1<<LOGN)+2), and the bench, run with a latency two clocks off, fails for every variant, so the passing runs prove the latency rather than assume it.

  • simulation test: pass The bench passes at the stated latency and fails two clocks off, for every variant: 2 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence

FBFP-F-BENCH: The bench catches seeded faults

Every one of the mutants in verif/mutants.yaml (one exact text change each: an inverted condition, a wrong constant, a dropped connection, a wrong width) makes the cocotb bench fail, or is argued equivalent in the file's header; mutants that survive or do not build are reported, never hidden.

  • simulation test: pass The mutation run as a test: no mutant survives, none fails to build: 1 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence
  • simulation measurement: pass Mutants killed of valid mutants (the record of the last full run): survived = []; invalid = []; valid = 23; score_pct = 100 evidence

FBFP-F-PACKAGE: The RTL is lint-clean, and its files agree with the specification and with the Vivado build files

spec.yaml states an interface contract (protocol, throughput, latency, reset, clock, stalls) and integration guidance (port timing, constraints to carry). Every variant passes Verilator -Wall at every point of spec.yaml; its ports and default parameters are the ones spec.yaml states; its header states the intended primitive use before any Vivado run; spec.md is the rendering of spec.yaml; the out-of-context wrapper and the build.yaml for each variant (Artix-7 xc7a100t and K26 xck26, prepared and not run) are current and lint-clean at every point they list, so a Vivado run can start from the repository alone.

  • simulation test: stale spec.yaml against the RTL headers, spec.md against spec.yaml, Verilator -Wall at every point, wrappers and build.yaml current and lint-clean: 5 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence

FBFP-F-ACCURACY: Accuracy against NumPy is measured beside the scaled and unscaled modes, and the stated bound holds

For both architectures, every N from 8 to 4096 and four input levels (0, -20, -40 and -60 dB of a full-scale noise-like frame), the error of the bit-exact model (which the RTL equals) against numpy.fft is recorded in verif/results/accuracy.json for the block-floating-point mode, the scaled mode and the unscaled mode (worst and rms error in the mode's own output LSB, and the SQNR). The bound is: the error of every part is at most the unscaled core's own worst error plus 2**exponent (the final rounding at the frame's exponent); it holds at every recorded point. The recorded SQNR of the block-floating-point mode at full level is at least 80 dB, its worst error at full level at most 2 output LSB, and at -60 dB its SQNR is at least 30 dB where the scaled mode's is negative.

  • simulation measurement: pass Error against NumPy at every recorded point, three modes (the record of the last run): all_within_bound = True; bfp_sqnr_db_min_full_level = 85.8; bfp_worst_lsb_max_full_level = 1.173; bfp_sqnr_db_min_minus60 = 35.4; scaled_sqnr_db_min_minus60 = -11; points = 80 evidence

FBFP-F-LEVELS: Every exponent range is visited and the exponent word is bit exact

Frames of very different level (all zero, one LSB, noise at six amplitudes, a loud and a quiet tone, the most negative value, a frame whose bin 0 is 2**W - 1 so that the rounding carries and the word saturates) are sent through both architectures; each output frame and its exponent equal the model, the frames reach at least six different exponents, and the exponent is the same on every word of its frame and travels with the data when frames follow each other at different levels.

  • simulation test: pass Frames of every level through the RTL, exponent included: 10 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence

FBFP-F-BACKPRESSURE: The burst variant honours out_ready, even when both frame banks fill

With out_ready low in 40 per cent of the clocks, and again in 92 per cent (reading a frame then takes longer than the core needs for the next, so both banks fill and the core must wait), the burst variant's frames, exponents and order equal the model, and out_valid, out_sof, the data and the exponent stay unchanged in every clock in which out_valid is high and out_ready low. The sdf variant ignores out_ready (stated).

  • simulation test: pass The burst variant under a stalled sink, including one that fills both frame banks: 4 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence

FBFP-F-HARNESS: The packaged harness runs standalone, passes on the repository's variants and catches faults

arith/fft_bfp/harness/ (model, bench, SystemVerilog checkers, coverage targets, standalone runner: owner decision 24) needs no other file of the repository. Run against each variant of this function with the checkers beside it, in every mode, it passes and meets every coverage target; against one planted fault (a mutant of verif/mutants.yaml), or with a latency two clocks off, it fails; its model, bench and checker files are the repository's own, generated by arith/tools/gen_harness_fft.py, so they cannot drift.

  • simulation test: pass The harness against the repository's variants, a planted fault and a wrong latency: 4 test(s) passed in the recorded run evidence
